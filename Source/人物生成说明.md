# 人物生成说明

使用内置 image_gen.imagegen，transparent_background=true；未使用 CLI。以下三次生成按顺序引用前一张图进行编辑，最终图集位于 Assets/poses-atlas.png。

## 初始提示词

Create a production sprite sheet PNG with genuinely transparent background. 4 columns by 2 rows, exactly eight equal cells, one full-body Cristiano Ronaldo in every cell, consistent photorealistic cutout human anatomy with humorously enlarged head 1.5x normal size (NOT cartoon or plastic). Portugal national football kit red shirt, green shorts, red socks, football boots. Camera fixed eye-level, studio soft lighting. All cells same character scale and centered feet baseline, ample transparent padding, no frames, no labels, no ground, no shadow outside character. Sequence row-major: 1 front facing relaxed standing arms down; 2 front facing crouch knees bent preparing jump; 3 front three-quarter facing left jumping arms rising above head; 4 left profile mid-air arms overhead; 5 rear three-quarter mid-air turning arms overhead; 6 direct BACK view landing legs wide and arms sweeping down outward; 7 direct BACK view iconic SIUUU celebration standing feet wide arms straight down diagonally out with spread fingers; 8 left profile relaxed standing for returning to front. Exact gold lettering RONALDO and large number 7 clearly readable on back in cells 5 6 7. Identity unmistakably Cristiano Ronaldo, short dark hair, athletic body, playful oversized head. Full figure visible in every cell, no cropped feet, no duplicates within cells. Prioritize consistency and crisp transparent cutout edges. This sprite atlas will be sliced programmatically into eight poses.

## 第二次：统一大头比例

Edit this eight-pose sprite atlas, keeping EXACT 4 columns 2 rows layout, poses and transparent background. This is a big-head Cristiano Ronaldo desktop pet. Match every pose's HEAD SIZE to the oversized head in the first front-facing pose: especially enlarge the heads in jumping poses 3,4,5 and rear celebration poses 6,7 and profile 8 so ALL have the same large bobblehead proportions as first pose. Keep photographic face likeness, Portuguese red jersey green shorts red socks boots, RONALDO and 7 gold lettering. Maintain equal anatomical body scale in all frames; arms raised should make a taller bounding box than standing at rest, while crouching is naturally shorter. Character fully inside each cell with transparent margins. No background, no vignette, no drop shadows. Do NOT change the first front pose. Keep all poses and camera directions. All eight figures fully visible with complete boots. Clean genuine alpha transparency.

## 第三次：修正图集间距（最终采用）

Fix ONLY layout/padding of this transparent sprite sheet. Keep the eight EXACT Cristiano Ronaldo big-head poses, the same photorealism, red Portugal kit, RONALDO 7 back. Output 4 columns x 2 rows sprite sheet, exactly eight equally sized cells. CRITICAL: Each complete figure must be scaled down slightly to fit FULLY inside its own cell with generous empty transparent padding of at least 25 pixels on ALL four sides. Heads, raised hands, shoes must NEVER touch any cell boundary, and poses must NEVER overlap into neighboring cells. Especially the fourth figure's raised hands and eighth figure's head and first figure's boots need empty margins. Maintain same anatomical scale across all eight figures. The first figure fullbody front idle; second front crouch; third threequarter jump arms raised; fourth profile jump arms raised; fifth rear-threequarter jump arms raised; sixth rear landing arms down-out; seventh rear SIUU feet spread arms down-out; eighth profile idle. Same bobblehead face as reference. Transparent background, no backdrop, no ground shadows, no labels, no grid lines. Keep every finger and boot intact.

## 构建

在 Windows PowerShell 中运行 Source/prepare.ps1 提取并对齐姿态，运行 Source/build.ps1 编译桌宠。素材目录已有裁好的音频；只有重新裁剪音频时才需要 Source/original-decoded.wav。

`RonaldoPet.exe --self-test` 检查动画时间线、素材与各尺寸 / DPI 渲染边界；`--render` 生成截图和 GIF；`--smoke-test` 启动短时集成检查并自动退出。
